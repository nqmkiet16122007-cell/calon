# BATTLE OF THE SEAS — 2D asset pack

Independent, animation-ready SVG assets for a calm underwater arcade survival game. Every animated family uses a stable viewBox and pivot.

## Contents

- PLAYER: 10 visibly growing levels, 8 swim frames and idle/level-up states per level.
- ENEMIES: six fish species in small/medium/large variants, with 8-frame swim loops.
- SHARK: small, medium and large versions with idle, swim, turn, attack, hurt and defeated states.
- HUD: 11 XP fill states (0-100%), full/half/empty heart icons, and pickup/active/8-frame states for shield, speed and magnet.
- ENVIRONMENT: shallow/mid/deep backgrounds, 10 trash types with 8 float/sink frames, modular rocks, coral, seaweed, ruins, ship props, shells and treasure.
- EFFECTS: 8-frame XP, level-up, ripple, pickup, damage, shield, speed and magnet effects.

## Audio direction

Ambient loop: 58-68 BPM, soft synth pad, gentle piano or marimba plucks, very quiet bubbles and distant water movement. Avoid percussion, dramatic hits and sharp high-frequency sounds.

All game art is vector SVG with no text embedded. See `manifest.json` for animation timing and pivots. The PNG production sheet one directory above is a visual overview; individual assets live here.
